**So, what is Maps.me, exactly?**
Maps.me is an offline maps and navigation app for Windows that gives travelers detailed maps and turn-by-turn directions without needing an active internet connection. It's built around OpenStreetMap data, so you can download an entire region ahead of time and navigate through it later with no mobile data or Wi-Fi required.

### Is Maps.me right for you?
**Traveling somewhere with patchy signal?** Maps.me's offline downloads mean the map still loads and routes still calculate. **Need directions on foot, by car, or by bike?** All three routing modes are built in. **Trying to find a nearby restaurant, hotel, or gas station?** The built-in point-of-interest search covers all of those categories.

> **What's next:** future updates are expected to keep refining the map data and search experience, though no specific features or dates are promised here.

### Will it run on my machine?

- [ ] Free storage space for the app plus whichever map regions you download

- [ ] Click the download button above to get the Maps.me installer.

### Okay, it's installed — now what?
The first launch is mostly about getting your first map region in place: search for or scroll to the area you care about, download it for offline use, and only then start searching for destinations or planning a route — everything after that is just searching, routing, and bookmarking as you normally would.
